# 《咚咚咚》storyboard

Logline: Clawd likes the girl in the window across the street (Blu), but can't say it out loud, so he blinks his
heartbeat across the street with a desk lamp, and she blinks back.

World: one night street with two facing windows (Clawd's on the left, Blu's on the right), a street lamp, a moon.
Palette: indigo night, warm ochre window light, rose hearts, teal for Blu.
Colour arc: a cold, dark street (only Blu's window lit) → Clawd's warm room → a street crossed by beams of light → a warm
night with both windows lit and a string of lights hung between them.

Motif: **light = heartbeat.** The kick drum is the heartbeat. The intro and verse use a "ba-dum" double kick, and the
chorus has a kick on every beat. Every kick drives the lamp flashes, the heart pumping, the camera bumps and the string
of lights. Each sung "咚" in the chorus is one big flash plus a ring.

Clawd's arc: lonely gazing → falls in love → shy (waves, then hides) → tongue-tied → an idea → bold (signals) → loved back.
Blu's arc: singing to herself → confused (who waved?) → surprised by the light → shy → in love → answers.

Music: 100 BPM, C major, one bar = 2.4 s. 0–9.6 intro | 9.6–28.8 verse | 28.8–48 chorus | 48–60 outro.

Shots:
  A  0–9.6   [in: iris opens on the street]  wide, dark street. Blu's window lights up on the bar-1 downbeat, and she
             pops up humming. The camera pans to Clawd's dark window as the heartbeat kicks start, and Clawd falls in
             love on a kick. It pushes in close; his chest heart pumps "ba-dum".
     reads:  0–2.4  night street, two windows (music box; stars twinkle on each note)
             2.4–4.4  Blu's light snaps on, and Blu pops up and hums
             4.4–5.6  the pan to Clawd (the heartbeat kicks start)
             6.0–9.0  Clawd: love take, then the heart pumping on the kicks
     [out: rose brush wipe]
  B  9.6–19.2  "隔着一条街的窗 / 亮着你的微光"  Clawd large in his window on the left, Blu's lit window small in the
             distance on the right. Clawd gazes, then waves (15.0). Blu turns his way (16.2), and Clawd panics, ducks
             under the sill and peeks out blushing. Blu: "?".
     [out: the curtains close (a curtain wipe)]
  C  19.2–28.8  "我想说的那句话 / 卡在心跳中央"  The curtains open on Clawd's room. He thinks, tries to speak (his mouth
             opens, but only a scribble comes out), then holds his giant pumping heart (the camera pushes in). During the
             snare roll he sees the desk lamp, gets an idea, trots over and grabs it.
     [out: a flash on the chorus downbeat]
  D  28.8–38.4  "咚咚咚 是我的心跳 / 一闪一闪 向你报到"  Clawd in his window blinks the lamp beam across the street on
             every kick. Each "咚" fires a big flash and ring. The camera rides the beam and whips right to Blu (33.4).
             "一闪一闪" is four blinks on her window. She is surprised, then shy, then in love, then grabs her own lamp.
  E  38.4–48  "咚咚咚 你听见了吗 / 我喜欢你 就在今晚"  Two-shot. Blu blinks back on each "咚", then they answer each
             other kick by kick. On "我喜欢你" the two beams meet in the middle and bloom into a heart of light. On
             "就在今晚" a string of lights pops out to both windows, one bulb per syllable, and they both jump.
  F  48–60  outro "咚咚咚 我喜欢你"  Wide street, rhyming with the opening: both windows lit now, the string of lights
             blinking with the heartbeat, the two swaying in sync. The camera pushes to the heart lantern.
     [out: a heart-shaped iris closes on the lantern after the last "ba-dum", then black]

Lyrics: the MV shows karaoke subtitles at the bottom of the frame. Each character lights up on its own note, so the
subtitles follow the vocal syllable by syllable.
